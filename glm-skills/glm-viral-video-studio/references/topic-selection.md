# Scoring and choosing topics

## 5-criteria matrix (1–5 points each)

| Criterion | 5 points when | 1 point when |
|---|---|---|
| Proven demand | ≥ 2 outliers ≥ 3× on different channels with the same topic | No evidence, gut feeling only |
| Competitive gap | There is an angle/format/market nobody does well | Dozens of near-identical videos, big channels dominate |
| Retention potential | Naturally strong hook + payoff/twist at the end | Flat content, no reason to watch to the end |
| Production cost | ≤ 100 Veo credits, phone-filmed, or fully renderable by the agent (mode B) | Needs > 400 credits or many Quality shots |
| Monetization potential | Matches the goal (high CPM, affiliate, own product) | Unrelated to any revenue source |

Maximum total is 25. Recommend the highest-scoring option; if the gap is ≤ 2 points, prefer the cheaper one (faster to make, more tests possible).

## Template for each option

```
### Option 1 — <Working title>  [Recommended]
Hook: "<opening line>"
Evidence: <outlier link> (x5.2), <link> (x3.8)
Differentiation: <why our video is different/better>
Scores: Demand 5 · Gap 4 · Retention 4 · Cost 5 · Monetization 4 = 22/25
Budget: ~120 Veo credits (6 Fast shots) — or 0 if self-filmed / rendered in mode B
```

## Tips for fast growth
- Prefer "remaking better" a proven outlier (new angle, local market, fresher data) over a brand-new idea with no evidence.
- Keep one format/hook for 4–6 videos before judging it, so the signal is stable enough.
- Topics that can become a series (part 1, 2, 3) build return-viewing habits.
