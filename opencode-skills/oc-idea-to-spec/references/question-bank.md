# Question Bank

Don't ask everything. Pick the questions with the biggest effect on revenue and design, skip anything research already answered, and attach a research-backed suggestion to each question. Order of priority: money (C, H) → problem (A) → differentiation (B) → scope (D) → the rest.

## A. Problem & customer
- Who has this problem most? Describe one specific person or company (role, size, income/budget, tools they use now).
- Is the user the same as the payer? Who approves the purchase?
- How do they solve it today, and what does that cost them in time or money?
- How often does the problem occur: daily / weekly / monthly / rarely?
- Have you experienced it yourself or talked to people who have? How many?

## B. Differentiation
- Finish this: "[Product] helps [who] do [what] better than [competitor/old way] because [reason]."
- Which competitor weakness (from the reviews found) will you fix?
- Any hard-to-copy advantage: proprietary data, audience, distribution, domain expertise, relationships, local knowledge?
- Why will this work now — what changed recently (tech, law, behavior, price of AI)?

## C. Money
- Who pays: end user, business, advertiser, third party?
- Model: subscription, usage-based, transaction fee/commission, one-time, freemium, paid pilot/setup fee, ads?
- Target price? (show competitor prices as anchors)
- Monthly revenue goal at 6 and 12 months? Minimum that makes this worth your time?
- Biggest variable cost per customer (AI tokens, SMS, hosting, support)? Does the margin survive it?
- Payment method your customers actually use? (cards, bank transfer/QR, e-wallets, invoices/net-30, app-store billing)
- Would you accept manual payments (bank transfer, invoice) for the first customers to launch faster?

## D. MVP scope
- The 3 things a user MUST be able to do in version 1?
- The "aha" moment — at which step does the user feel the value?
- What can wait? What will you never build?
- Target date for first paying customer?

## E. Experience & flows
- Where do users come from, and what do they do in their first 5 minutes?
- Sign-in method (email/magic link, Google, phone OTP, Zalo, SSO)?
- Roles (owner, staff, customer, admin)?
- Languages, currencies, time zones?

## F. Technical
- Platform: web, iOS, Android, desktop, API, chat bot (Zalo/Telegram/Messenger/WhatsApp), browser extension?
- Preferred stack or existing skills?
- Core data and expected volume in year 1?
- Integrations: payments, email/SMS, AI models, maps, file storage, accounting, CRM?
- Offline, real-time, push notifications needed?
- Hosting preference and monthly infrastructure budget cap?

## G. Non-functional & legal
- Sensitive data (personal, health, financial, children)?
- Applicable rules — verify current status during research. Examples: Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection No. 91/2025/QH15 (effective 2026-01-01) and its guiding decree; GDPR (EU users); CCPA/CPRA (California); HIPAA (US health); PCI DSS (card data — avoid by using a hosted checkout).
- Performance, uptime, backup expectations?

## H. Distribution & resources
- Where exactly will the first 10 paying customers come from? Name the community, list, channel or contacts.
- Existing audience or network (followers, newsletter, industry contacts)?
- Budget for ads/marketing? Willing to do direct outreach/sales?
- Hours per week and runway (months you can work on this without income)?
- Side income, bootstrapped business, or venture-funded startup?

## Type-specific add-ons
- **Marketplace:** which side is harder to acquire? How to solve chicken-and-egg (seed supply manually, single niche/city)? How to prevent off-platform deals? Take rate?
- **AI app/agent:** acceptable error rate and what happens when the AI is wrong? Token cost per user per month vs. price? Why won't customers just use ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini directly — what workflow, data or integration makes it sticky?
- **B2B SaaS:** sales cycle length? Need SSO, roles, audit logs, invoices? Per-seat or per-usage pricing?
- **Consumer/game:** retention loop? Viral/referral mechanic? Monetization (subscription, IAP, ads) and app-store fee impact?
- **Dev tool:** open-source vs. closed? Monetize via hosted version, team features, or enterprise?
- **Bot/extension:** platform policy risk and dependency on a single platform?
